Journal

About the collection

Every frame here was kept because it stopped a scroll or a walk for a second longer than expected. There's no single subject running through it — a doorway, a coastline, a stranger's hands — only the habit of looking twice.

Spring

Started the habit of carrying a camera on ordinary errands, not just planned outings.

Summer

Most of the street and portrait frames came from this stretch — longer days, more strangers.

Autumn

Slowed down. Fewer frames, but the architecture and abstract shots sharpened here.

Winter

Went back through everything and let the collection settle into what you see now.

Stay in the light

A short note whenever a new volume drops. No noise in between.
